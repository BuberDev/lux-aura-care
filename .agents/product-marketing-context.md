# Lux Aura Care Product Marketing Context

Lux Aura Care is a premium beauty ecommerce store. Product pages must feel like a
professional, high-trust shopping experience for Polish customers, with clear
purchase support and no placeholder-like UI.

Core standards:

- Every interactive visual cue must be honest. A play icon must play a video or
  open a clearly identifiable video player.
- UGC should build confidence by showing real product use, not by adding generic
  decorative thumbnails.
- Customer-facing labels must be polished and descriptive. Avoid numbered labels
  such as "Film klientki 01" or "Video 02" unless they are part of a deliberate
  editorial series.
- Polish copy should prioritize concrete beauty ecommerce language: "masaz
  twarzy", "pielegnacja", "roller", "gua sha", "serum", "olejek",
  "zestaw", "w uzyciu".
- Use "rytual" sparingly. It can support brand mood, but repeated use sounds
  translated and weakens sales clarity.
- Product pages should answer shopper questions quickly: what is included, how
  it is used, what the finish looks like, what the shopper should choose, and
  what happens after clicking the CTA.
- The design bar is professional store quality: strong media, clear hierarchy,
  real controls, accessible interactions, mobile-first behavior, and no filler
  content.

Research anchors:

- Baymard product-page video research: videos must be discoverable near the
  product overview/gallery and must not be hidden far down the page.
- Nielsen Norman Group product-page guidance: images and videos should answer
  shopper questions and show product use/context, not act as decoration.
- Baymard review/UGC research: real-life customer or creator media improves
  confidence when it gives specific insight into the product.
